Exhibition notes

SF—07 / Ongoing

A museum with no walls, viewed through a piece of living glass.

Soft Focus is a typographic playground about the tension between clarity and distortion. Every object changes when the lens passes over it.

The glass is rendered in WebGL. It bends the underlying image using a spherical surface normal, wavelength-dependent refraction, and view-angle reflections.
